Mini-league bot · Field guide

Stop opening ten team pages

There's a bot in this group chat now. It watches our mini-league during matches and answers with the whole table in one message — live, while the FPL app is still hours behind.

01

Get set up

  1. Nothing, if you just want to read

    Every command works for everyone the moment the bot joins. You only need the next two steps if you want the bot to know which team is yours.

  2. Find your team ID

    Open the FPL site or app, go to Points or My Team, and look at the web address. It contains /entry/1234567/ — that number is you.

  3. Claim it in the chat

    Send /me 1234567. Now the bot can tag you by name when your players score, and /diff knows who to compare you against.

It never asks for your FPL password, and it can't see your team before the deadline. Everything it reads is the public data anyone can see on the league page. Unsubmitted teams are hidden by FPL itself, so the bot literally cannot look.

The live table. Points, season totals, players left.

Who still has bullets — split into on the pitch now and yet to kick off.

Who captained whom, and what the armband has actually returned.
/bench
Points rotting on people's benches. Reliably the funniest message of the weekend.
/diff
The rare picks — players only one or two of us own. That's where the gameweek is won.
/diff wei
Head-to-head. Exactly which players separate you and one specific person, and who's winning that battle.
/eo haaland
Effective ownership. If nine of us own him and six captain him, a haul doesn't help you — a blank ruins everyone else. This is the number that tells you whether to sweat.

06

Turning the volume down

Send /settings and pick a level. It applies to the whole group, so agree on it between you.

all ≈ 25–30 messages a gameweek

Every goal, assist, booking and red card involving a player we own. Best if the chat exists mainly for the football.

big-moments ≈ 12–18 · the default

Goals, red cards, lead changes, deadlines and the full-time results. Skips assists and bookings.

digest-only ≈ 4–5

Nothing during matches. Deadline reminders, the full-time table and the wrap-up, and that's it.

off 0

Completely silent. Every command still works when someone asks.

Quiet hours

Set to 1am–8am by default. Evening kick-offs in England finish around 6am here, and nobody wants that. Anything that happens in the window is held and delivered as one summary in the morning.

Deadline reminders follow the same rule, which is why you'll usually get them around 7:30pm and 11:30pm the night before rather than at 1:15am, fifteen minutes before the actual deadline.

07

Questions

Do I have to give it my FPL login?

No, and it will never ask. Everything it uses is public data that anyone can pull from the league page. If something ever asks you for your FPL password, it isn't this bot.

Can it see my team before the deadline?

No. FPL hides unsubmitted teams from everyone, so the bot has no way to look even if it wanted to. Picks only become visible after the deadline passes, which is the same moment you can see everyone else's.

Why doesn't this match the table in the FPL app?

Because the FPL app's mini-league table doesn't update live — it refreshes in batches and can be several hours behind during a match day. The bot adds the scores up itself from live match data, so it's current to the minute. When they disagree mid-Saturday, the bot is the one that's right.

What does the little ↑ arrow mean?

An automatic substitution the bot expects to happen. If one of your starters played zero minutes and his match is over, FPL will eventually sub in a bench player — but it only does that once every game in the gameweek has finished. The bot shows you the projected final score now and marks it with ↑ so you know it isn't official yet.

It's too noisy. / It's too quiet.

Send /settings. Four levels, from every booking to complete silence. Changing it affects everyone in this chat, not just you.

Can the same bot run in my other FPL group?

Yes. Add it to that chat and run /link with that league's ID. Each group gets its own league, its own volume setting and its own quiet hours — they don't interfere with each other. One chat can even follow several leagues at once and switch between them with /leagues.

Where do I find the league ID?

Open the mini-league on the FPL site. The address contains /leagues/123456/standings/ — that number. Whoever sets the bot up in a chat runs /link 123456 once and everyone else is covered.

Nothing happened when I sent a command.

Two usual causes. Either no league is linked to the chat yet — someone needs to run /link first — or you sent several commands in a few seconds and got rate-limited, which sorts itself out after ten seconds. If a gameweek hasn't started, /live will show the last completed one.
